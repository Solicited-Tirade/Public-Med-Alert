The problem

Rescues happen out of sight

Leaky sharingClients already post clips and screenshots, with names, handles and locations left in.
No verified recordThere is no trusted source for what actually happened on a rescue.
Crew exposureMembers can be identified by someone else's post, without a say.
Unseen good workGreat rescues go unnoticed. A verified record shows what Medrunner does.
03

The idea

An opt-in record, reviewed before it goes public

01 · ConsentNothing is published unless the client opts in. They control their own messages.
02 · ReviewThe attending team lead checks everything. Crew can advise. Staff oversee.
03 · PrivacyCrew appear only by role. Sensitive details are detected and redacted. Takedowns stay possible.
04

How it works

Six steps from discharge to public record

  1. Client requestOpt in after discharge
  2. Team lead ping72h Discord window
  3. Crew advisoriesSuggestions only
  4. ReviewRedact and decide
  5. StaffEscalations and takedowns
  6. Public recordVerified, by role
05

Step 1 · Client

The client decides

  • Rate the crew and hand out up to three kudos.
  • Optional testimonial, with live warnings for handles, links and locations.
  • Hide any of their own messages, with one or more reasons.
  • Agree that their RSI handle is shown, then submit.
06

Step 3 · Crew

Crew advise, the team lead decides

  • Same tools as the team lead: highlight text to suggest a redaction.
  • Suggest hiding a whole message, with reasons.
  • Advise withholding the mission, or leave a note.
  • Nothing changes until the team lead accepts it.
07

Step 4 · Team lead

Review and redact

  • Auto-detected suggestions: nicknames, Discord usernames, links, banned names.
  • Crew advisories in one panel, each with Accept or Decline.
  • Highlight to redact, including every mention at once.
  • Approve, reject, send back or escalate. Every action is logged.
08

Step 5 · Staff

Oversight built in

  • Review queue shows time left on each export and what is in the leadership pool.
  • Escalation and takedown desk. Every decision needs a reason.
  • Audit trail of every hide, redaction and decision.
  • Abuse controls: pause export rights, ban list for auto-detection.
09

Step 6 · Public

The public record

  • Outcome-aware design, including a respectful card for bad outcomes.
  • Crew shown by role, redactions shown as categories.
  • Kudos, testimonial and positive crew milestones.
  • Share card, takedown link, and an emergency button to the portal.
10

Privacy and safety

Safe by default

  • Consent first: the client opts in and can hide their own messages.
  • Roles, not names: crew are swapped for roles automatically.
  • Auto-detection: Discord usernames, links, hab locations, profanity.
  • Reasons everywhere: every redaction, hide and decision is explained.
  • Time-boxed: 72h review, then the leadership pool.
  • Reversible: takedown requests at any time, decided by staff.
