Public mission export: interactive showcase

Follow one alert from discharge to a published record. Every action is simulated and logged, and nothing is sent anywhere. Pick a scenario, switch who you're viewing as, and click around.

Important: showcase, not a spec

  • • This shows what could be possible. It is not final and not an exhaustive list.
  • • Not all features will fit. Not all of them can, or should, be implemented.
  • • All aspects are in development. Treat every feature here as an idea to discuss, not a commitment.
